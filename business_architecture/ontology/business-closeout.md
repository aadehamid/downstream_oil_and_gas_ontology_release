# Business close-out package

This package lists the points about the ontology that only the business can settle. `OPEN-ITEMS.md` lists everything open on the ontology, these points among them. a subject matter expert reviewing the documents asked for this package on 2026-10-23, so that it goes with the ontology and lists what to close out with the business (the decision of 2026-10-23). Each point is settled when the ontology is first shared with business consumers, unless a row says otherwise. *2026-11-02:* the follow-up release, that first share, may be cut with point 1 (Q17) still open, listed in `OPEN-ITEMS.md` (the decision of 2026-10-23, amendment). The work that does not wait on the business is in the decision of 2026-10-23: six approved releases, which carry MPC-P10 and the product values and close the 16 projection gaps. Until they merge, the README's Status section says which have landed. *2026-10-23:* all six are released and tagged (the decisions of 2026-10-23 to 2026-10-23), and the ontology is closed (the decision of 2026-10-23); the points below stay open with the business. *2026-10-26:* the ontology is done (the decision of 2026-10-26). Points 1 and 2 stay open by the decision owner's choice, and the decision owner may work through some of the others first, to narrow them down. *2026-10-28:* the decision owner worked through points 3 to 16 (the decision of 2026-10-23, amendment of 2026-10-28). Points 3 and 4 are closed, point 5 is narrowed to the PIDX family letters, and points 17 and 18 are added from the decision of 2026-10-27. The answers to points 7, 8, 9, 11 and 15 change the ontology, so each row says so until its release merges. Points 7, 8 and 9 are released in `organization` 2.2.0, and points 7 and 8 are closed. Points 11 and 15 are released in `party` 2.1.0, and both stay open for what is left (the decision of 2026-10-25, amendment of 2026-10-28).

Each row gives the point, what the ontology holds today, what the business needs to say, and the record that holds the answer. Settling a point changes no released IRI unless the row says so. The answer is recorded as an amendment to the named record, in the business's own words, and goes into the next release of the module named.
