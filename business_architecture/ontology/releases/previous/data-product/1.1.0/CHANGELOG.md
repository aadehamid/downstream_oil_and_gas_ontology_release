## 1.0.0 (2026-10-22)

The module's first release, a major. Approved by the decision owner with the release plan ("release plan approved", saved in (the review comments of a subject matter expert reviewing the documents); the decision of 2026-10-22). It holds the portfolio's 3 segments, 19 product lines and 48 data products as definitions, never data values (the decision of 2026-10-05, the decision of 2026-10-19). The 15 Commercial data products are defined from the portfolio's descriptions; the 33 Midstream and Corporate data products are in the package as parked items, each with a note that its definition waits for its data owners, because the portfolio names them without a description. Loss Control is produced by CM 1.1.3.7.13 (the decision of 2026-10-19). `dp:DataProduct` is mapped to `dcat:Dataset`, not imported. It requires core 2.2.0.
