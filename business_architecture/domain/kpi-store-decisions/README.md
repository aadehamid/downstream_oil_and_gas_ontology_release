# KPI Store decision records

Each record states one decision, the owner's words that made it, and what the agent did with it. The owner's words
are saved in `answers/`, and a record quotes them from there.

| Record | Decision | Status | Date |
|---|---|---|---|
| [KPI-DEC-0001](KPI-DEC-0001.md) | KPI Store 1.1 is a stand-alone release for one platform, built under a recorded delegation | Decided (the owner) | 2026-11-11 |

A decision the agent makes under the delegation in KPI-DEC-0001 is recorded where its question lives, marked
"Decided (delegated under KPI-DEC-0001)". The owner can reverse any of them.
